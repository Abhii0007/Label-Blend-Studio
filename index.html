Your Privacy Matters

Label Blend is designed as a privacy-focused desktop application. Your images, datasets, annotations, and projects remain on your own computer unless you explicitly choose to export or share them.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Label Blend ("Software"), developed by VermaAgentix, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

Label Blend does not require user accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information while using the software offline.

The application does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Payment information
  • Location information
  • Biometric information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or tracking information

3. Images and Datasets

Label Blend processes images, annotations, datasets, and project files locally on your computer.

  • Your original images remain yours.
  • Your generated datasets remain yours.
  • Your annotations remain yours.
  • Your exported files remain yours.

Label Blend does not upload your files to VermaAgentix servers unless an optional online feature is explicitly enabled by you.

4. Artificial Intelligence Features

Label Blend includes AI-assisted features that may help generate synthetic datasets, perform annotation assistance, object labeling, image processing, and segmentation.

AI-generated results are intended as assistance only. Users remain responsible for reviewing generated annotations before using them in research or production.

5. Internet Connectivity

The primary functionality of Label Blend works completely offline.

Future optional online features, if introduced, will clearly indicate when an internet connection is required.

6. Data Storage

Application settings, projects, generated datasets, and annotations are stored locally on your computer.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of your own data.

7. Third-Party Components

Label Blend uses open-source software libraries to provide functionality. These components are governed by their respective licenses.

8. Children's Privacy

Label Blend is intended for developers, researchers, students, educators, and professionals working with computer vision datasets.

The application is not specifically intended for children under 13 years of age.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to develop and distribute Label Blend securely. However, no software system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
